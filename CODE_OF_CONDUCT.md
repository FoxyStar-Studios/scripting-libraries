As members of our community, we pledge to make participation in our projects a safe, welcoming, and respectful experience for everyone.

## Rules
- **Be respectful** — Our team includes people of all ages, nationalities, races, and religions. Slurs, targeted insults, and advocacy of harm are not tolerated.  
- **Keep it appropriate** — Gore, sexual content, and other explicit material are strictly prohibited.  
- **Behave responsibly** — No spam, harassment, or sharing of personal/private information.  
- **Be constructive** — Feedback and discussions should aim to help the project and each other.  

## Scope
These rules apply *everywhere*, and to *everyone*, including team members. This includes, but is not limited to:
- GitHub
  - Issues
  - Pull requests
  - Discussions
- Discord
- Any other official community spaces

---

## Enforcement
While we can't monitor every angle of our communities at all time, private reports to contact@foxystar.net are appreciated.

Violations may result in warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal from the project and community spaces, depending on severity.

---

By participating in this community, you agree to uphold these standards and help us maintain a positive, inclusive environment.
